Associate Processor at the [Chengdu University of Information Technology (CUIT)](https://cas.cuit.edu.cn/info/1018/1946.htm) in Chengdu, China. My research focuses on stratospheric transport and redistribution of important trace gases, like ozone and water vapour.

I am the community lead of the WCRP APARC activity [QUOCA (QUasi-biennial oscillation and Ozone Chemistry interactions in the Atmosphere)](https://sites.google.com/cam.ac.uk/quoca-project/working-groups?authuser=0). This is a new joint QBOi-CCMI activity aimed at improving understanding of QBO-ozone feedbacks in present-day and future climates. If you are interested in contributing to this new activity, please get in touch.

You can download my CV [here](/XinZhou_CV_May2025.pdf), view my publications on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Xin-Zhou-57/research), and find my ORCID record [here](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5311-0092).

## Research Interests

- Atmospheric impacts of Volcanic eruption
- Risks & benifits of Stratospheric Aerosol Injection
- Age of stratospheric air and long-lived trace gases
