I am a researcher at the [Chengdu University of Information Technology (CUIT)](https://cas.cuit.edu.cn/info/1018/1946.htm) in Chengdu, China. My research focuses on computational methods and data-driven approaches for engineering problems, including heat transfer modeling, hydrological prediction, and applied machine learning.

You can download my CV [here](/XinZhou_CV_May2025.pdf), view my publications on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Xin-Zhou-57/research), and find my ORCID record [here](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5311-0092).

## Research Interests

- Hydrothermal heat transfer modeling and prediction
- Deep learning and machine learning for scientific computing
- Time-series forecasting and signal processing
- Numerical simulation and computational fluid dynamics
