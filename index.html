About Me

Associate Professor at the Chengdu University of Information Technology (CUIT). My research focuses on stratospheric transport and redistribution of important trace gases, like ozone and water vapour.

I am the community lead of the WCRP APARC activity QUOCA (QUasi-biennial oscillation and Ozone Chemistry interactions in the Atmosphere. This is a new joint QBOi-CCMI activity aimed at improving understanding of QBO-ozone feedbacks in present-day and future climates. If you are interested in contributing to this new activity, please get in touch.

You can download my CV here, or view my full publication list on ResearchGate.

Research Interests

My research group consists of 8 postgraduate students (3-year, M.Res.). Our current research projects include:

  • Atmospheric impacts of volcanic eruptions (China NSFC-UK Royal Society international exchange programme)
  • Risks & benefits of Stratospheric Aerosol Injection (MOST)
  • Studing changes in the mean age of stratospheric air and long-lived trace gases (NSFC)
  • Dynamics and predictability in stratosphere-troposphere interaction (NSFC)
