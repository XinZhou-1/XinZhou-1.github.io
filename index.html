About Me

I am a researcher at the Chengdu University of Information Technology (CUIT). My research focuses on computational methods and data-driven approaches for engineering problems, with a particular interest in heat transfer modeling, hydrological prediction, and applied machine learning.

I am always open to collaborations and discussions. Feel free to reach out by email or connect through any of the channels listed on the left.

You can download my CV here, or view my full publication list on ResearchGate.

Research Interests

My work spans several interconnected areas at the interface of data science and engineering:

  • Hydrothermal heat transfer modeling and prediction
  • Deep learning and machine learning for scientific computing
  • Time-series forecasting and signal processing
  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and numerical simulation
